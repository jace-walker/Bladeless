Face check-in

Check in to club events just by looking at a camera.

Check in Already added your face? Start here. Add my face First time? It takes about a minute.

How it works

  1. Add your face once. Type your name, say OK, and take 5 pictures.
  2. Look at the camera when you arrive at an event.
  3. You're checked in. No sign-in sheet, no QR code.

Running an event? Open the organizer page to pick the event and see who's here.

About this demo

A hackathon project: members add their face once, then check in to club events by looking at a camera. It's built as a feature for Blade, our club's member portal, but it runs on its own with made-up members.

How it works

  1. The check-in screen takes a snapshot from the camera about twice a second.
  2. YuNet, OpenCV's face detector, finds each face near the camera.
  3. Facenet512, a neural network, turns each face into 512 numbers. The picture is then thrown away.
  4. Neon Postgres, with the pgvector extension, finds the closest saved face. If it's close enough (a distance of 0.30 or less), that member is checked in.

Privacy

  • Only the numbers are saved, never pictures.
  • Members agree before their face is saved, and can delete it any time.
  • This demo deletes everyone's face data every night and goes back to its made-up members.

Built with

  • Flask (Python) on Vercel
  • Neon Postgres with pgvector
  • DeepFace, running YuNet and Facenet512, with model weights from the Hugging Face Hub

Team
