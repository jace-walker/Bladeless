Ask the member to read this

Face check-in lets the door camera recognize you, so you don’t have to sign in by hand.

  • The app saves numbers that describe your face, not photos, in the club’s check-in database.
  • Camera frames go over HTTPS to the check-in server (hosted on Vercel), are processed in memory, and are never saved.
  • You can ask an organizer to delete your face data at any time. Your attendance history stays.
